Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Buzaar ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Buzaar mobile application. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address, display name, and handle when you register.
  • Profile information: optional avatar color, location (city), Instagram and Facebook handles you choose to add.
  • Content you post: bounty listings, offers, and messages sent through the offer thread.
  • Photos: images you upload for bounty listings, stored in Supabase Storage.
  • Device location: approximate location (with your permission) to show nearby events. Never stored on our servers.
  • Push token: an Expo push token to send you notifications about your bounties and offers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the app — matching collectors with sellers at events.
  • To send push notifications about offer activity on your bounties.
  • To display your public profile to other Buzaar users.
  • To enforce our community guidelines and prevent abuse.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on Supabase servers. We do not sell your data to third parties.

4. Photos

Photos you upload are stored in a public Supabase Storage bucket and are accessible via URL. Do not upload photos containing sensitive personal information.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — database and authentication (privacy policy)
  • Expo — push notification delivery (privacy policy)
  • Google Maps — event venue maps (privacy policy)

6. Your Rights

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. You can also update your profile information from within the app.

7. Children

Buzaar is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email.

9. Contact

Questions? Email us at support@thepixelclub.app
